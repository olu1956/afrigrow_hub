# AfriGrow Hub — Partner Programme

**One-pager for prospective partners** · Version 1.0 · July 2026

## Why partner with AfriGrow

AfriGrow Hub is the growth platform for African and Black-owned SMEs — profiles, marketing, matching, funding tools, and CRM in one place.

Partners reach founders at the moment they are building, promoting, and looking for tools. Every featured partner **must give AfriGrow members a benefit they cannot get on the public website**. That member value is the foundation of the programme; sponsorship and revenue share sit on top when billing is live.

## What we expect from partners (minimum)

1. **Member-exclusive offer** — discount, extended trial, free consult, waived fee, or priority access, redeemable only via AfriGrow (promo code or unique link).
2. **Logo + short copy** — approved brand assets and a 1–2 sentence description for the partners page.
3. **Tracking** — unique URL, promo code, or referral ID (required for Featured and Premium; strongly preferred for apps and fintech).
4. **Co-promotion** — at least one mention when listed (social, newsletter, or community channel); Premium: quarterly cadence.
5. **Offer validity** — keep the member offer live for the agreement term; notify AfriGrow 30 days before changing or ending it.

Community partners without a commercial product may substitute **events, referrals, or content** for a discount — still subject to approval.

## Agreement bullets (term sheet)

Non-binding summary for discussion; a short written agreement will follow acceptance.

1. **Term** — 12 months from go-live on the partners page; auto-renew unless either party gives 30 days’ written notice.
2. **Placement** — AfriGrow assigns Community / Featured / Premium based on offer strength and inventory; Premium placements are limited.
3. **Member offer** — Partner warrants the advertised exclusive is available to AfriGrow members for the full term via the agreed code or link.
4. **Creative approval** — AfriGrow may edit copy for length and clarity; Partner approves logo and legal name use once at onboarding (and on material change).
5. **Fees** — During early access, cash fees are waived unless otherwise agreed. When SME billing is live, Featured/Premium fees or affiliate rates apply as set out in the schedule; Community remains fee-optional.
6. **Affiliate / reporting** — Where rev-share applies, Partner provides monthly conversion counts for the AfriGrow code/link; AfriGrow may share anonymised click stats from the site.
7. **Brand use** — Each party may use the other’s name and logo solely to promote the partnership; no implication of equity, employment, or exclusive agency.
8. **No exclusivity (default)** — Category exclusivity is available only on Premium by written rider.
9. **Conduct** — Offers and marketing must be lawful, accurate, and appropriate for AfriGrow’s SME audience; AfriGrow may pause or remove a listing for material breach or reputational risk, with notice where practical.
10. **Data** — No sale of member personal data. Leads generated via partner CTAs are handled under each party’s privacy policy; Partner processes only what users submit to Partner.
11. **Independence** — Partnership does not create a joint venture; each party remains responsible for its own products, pricing, and support.
12. **Termination** — Either party may terminate for convenience with 30 days’ notice, or immediately for material breach not cured within 14 days; fees already paid for the current month are non-refundable unless AfriGrow removes Partner without cause.

## Quick FAQ

**Do we pay during free early access?**  
Usually no, if the exclusive member offer (or Community co-promo) is in place. Paid sponsorship starts when AfriGrow charges members — or earlier by mutual agreement for Premium brands that want guaranteed inventory.

**Can we be listed with only a logo?**  
No. Every Partner badge needs member value or approved Community co-marketing.

**What if our offer changes?**  
Give 30 days’ notice so AfriGrow can update the card; material reductions may trigger a tier review.
